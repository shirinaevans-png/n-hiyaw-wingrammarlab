nêhiyawêwin Grammar Lab

A prototype learning resource for exploring Plains Cree vocabulary and grammatical categories through play. Built from the project vocabulary workbook.

Learn grammar by noticing patterns

This first version focuses on four things the workbook can support safely: noun animacy, verb classes, vocabulary recognition, and a verified VAI person/number paradigm.

Animacy Challenge

Decide whether a noun is grammatically animate (NA) or inanimate (NI).

Verb Detective

Identify VAI, VTA, VTI, and VII forms using the grammatical labels already in the vocabulary data.

Word Match

Match a Cree form to its English meaning.

Sentence Builder

Explore verified independent-order forms of mîcisow “eat.”

Design rule: this prototype does not automatically invent conjugations for every verb. The workbook itself warns that Cree verb classes and stem patterns cannot be treated as one universal ending system. Later versions can add only paradigms that have been verified.
